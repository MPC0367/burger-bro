## Full menu page

- **Route:** `menu/index.html`, a real file. Works at `/burger-bro/menu/` and at a root `/menu/` (relative paths only); direct open, reload and Back/Forward tested under both bases; every asset resolves (no 4xx); "Back to Burger Bro" lands on each base's home. No modal, no `#hash` route, no 404 trick.
- **Same rows:** the homepage's own stylesheet (generated copy `menu/home.css`) and row markup; row type compared in the browser (font family, size, weight, colour, case) on both pages — identical.
- **Discovery:** title, Back to Burger Bro, branch selector, **Search menu** (EN and TH text, stable ids such as `burger-bro` and aliases such as `b1`; Thai text found from the English page; missing translations fall back to English, never hide a dish), category buttons (All, the five categories, Value sets), protein filters built from the data (Beef, Pork, Chicken, Fish & shrimp), **Reset filters**, result count, empty state naming the search and offering Reset.
- **Details:** a visible button on every row ("View photo" for photo rows, "Details" otherwise) opens a native dialog with photo, description, price basis and proteins; tap, click and keyboard (Enter) tested; Escape, Close and the backdrop close it; focus returns to its button; it has its own history entry (Back closes it). "Order this" closes it and moves to ordering, with "Find <dish> in the delivery app and add it there — nothing is added for you from this site."
- **Ordering:** the same branch-aware LINE MAN / GrabFood hand-off as the homepage (Rama 9's verified Grab link and computer QR; Pattanakarn's "Find Pattanakarn on Grab" with its note), "Call, walk in or see the map →" to the homepage's `#order`. No basket.
- **Shareable state:** `?q=`, `?cat=`, `?protein=`, `?item=`, `?branch=`, `?lang=`. Typing replaces the address entry (one history entry per search); category and protein changes are history steps; Back/Forward restore them (tested).
- **Not loaded on this page:** the map, the burger sequence and the arcade (network log checked; the arcade imports only if Win a coupon is pressed).

## Shared context

`shared/site.js` (`BBPrefs.resolve`) is the one resolver for language and branch on both pages: a valid address value, then the saved choice, then the default; invalid values ignored. Tested: saved Thai + `?lang=en` → English on both pages; `?branch=nonsense&lang=xx` → saved/default; a TH + Pattanakarn menu link opens as shared and its "Back to Burger Bro" opens the homepage in TH with Pattanakarn; choosing Pattanakarn on the menu page is saved and the homepage then opens on it.

## Burger story (B01–B05)

- **Look:** warm paper `#F5EEDF`, a soft static cream/mustard radial glow behind the burger, the page's existing 5% paper grain, a soft grounded shadow, a 1px edge and 6px radius on the photograph; no poster border or offset shadow. Compact heading ("ORDER № 149 · OUR SIGNATURE — ฿149", then "THE BURGER BRO, TOP TO BOTTOM." at 26–38px instead of a second giant intro). Anton, Libre Franklin 16–18px / 1.55, Space Mono labels and Kanit for Thai — the fonts the site already loads.
- **One state:** progress 0…1 drives the frame, the active ingredient (title + description), the counter and the chapter buttons. Chapter checkpoints are frames 0, 12, 24, 36, 48 — the frames do not isolate single layers (inspected: the sequence opens the whole burger steadily), so the five chapters sit at even points; tested at every viewport that each chapter button shows its frame (±1) and its ingredient together.

## B06 — manual branch choice is authoritative

Every branch choice on the homepage goes through `chooseBranch(i, origin)` (origins: tab, marker, footer, panel, nearest, url, saved, location); the menu page's selector goes through its own `selectBranch(id, "menu-selector")`, saving to the same preference. Deliberate choices bump a counter; a location answer applies only if the counter has not moved since it was asked. Tested: Nearest to me with a 2.5 s answer next to Rama 9, then Pattanakarn chosen by **map marker** (the styled map loaded in both engines) and, separately, by **tab** → after the answer, the tab, Find us panel, LINE MAN link, hidden Call button, Directions and the address all stay on Pattanakarn. Also: a saved branch is not overridden by a quietly reused location; with nothing chosen, an already-granted location is reused quietly.
